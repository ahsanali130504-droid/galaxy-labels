Our Facility

The Cutting & Folding Unit

What It Does

Once labels are woven or printed, they come off the loom or press as one continuous roll of fabric. This machine's job is to take that roll and turn it into individual, ready-to-use labels — cutting each one to size and folding it into the exact shape required.

Why Folding Matters

Not every garment needs the same kind of label finish. Some require the label folded in half so the edges are hidden inside a seam, others need the raw edges tucked under, and some are meant to sit flat. This unit handles all of these fold styles automatically, switching setups depending on the order.

Consistency Is Key

Imagine trying to cut ten thousand labels by hand — no two would come out exactly the same size. This machine removes that variability entirely. Whether it's the first label off the roll or the ten thousandth, every single piece comes out identical in size, shape, and fold.

Clean Finish

The cutting mechanism trims each edge cleanly, with no loose threads or ragged corners. That clean finish matters — it's often the difference between a label that looks handmade and one that looks genuinely professional once it's sewn onto a finished product.

Handles Large Orders Easily

Because the whole process is automated, this unit can process large volumes in a fraction of the time manual cutting would take — which is exactly what's needed for bulk orders with tight delivery timelines.
