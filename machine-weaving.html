Our Facility

The Weaving Loom

What It Does

Our jacquard weaving loom is where every woven label begins its journey. Instead of printing a design onto fabric, this machine builds the design out of thread itself — weaving your logo, text, and pattern directly into the material, line by line, with remarkable precision.

Why This Method Is Better

Because the design is woven in rather than printed on top, it becomes part of the fabric's structure. That means it can't peel off, crack, or fade the way a printed surface eventually can. Even after hundreds of wash cycles and years of everyday wear, a woven label still holds its shape, color, and detail.

The Feel & Look

There's a reason woven labels are associated with premium clothing brands — the texture. A woven label has a soft, fabric-like feel against the skin instead of the stiffer, rougher surface that printed labels can have. It's a small detail, but customers notice it, and it shapes how they perceive the quality of the garment.

What We Make With It

This machine handles everything from brand logo labels and size tags to care instruction labels and woven badges. Whether it's a large batch for a big order or a smaller custom run, the loom keeps the output consistent — every piece matching the last.

Speed & Precision

Modern jacquard technology allows the loom to weave intricate, detailed designs at high speed without sacrificing sharpness. Even small text or fine logo details come out clean and legible, which is critical for labels that often measure just a few centimeters across.
